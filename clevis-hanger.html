    Clevis Hangers for Suspended Pipework

    The clevis hanger is one of the most widely used supports for hanging horizontal pipework from a threaded rod. A U-shaped lower strap cradles the pipe and a cross bolt connects it to the upper strap, so the line can be levelled and adjusted after installation.

    We supply 15 sizes from 1/2" to 14", each with a 2D drawing and a dimension table showing rod size, rod out, overall height and adjustment. They are used throughout building services, sprinkler systems, plant rooms and process plants, in stainless steel, galvanised or carbon steel.

    Everything in this range is supplied to order by Ensign Manufacturing in your choice of size, material and finish. Choose a product above, or contact us with your requirements or a drawing for anything non-standard.
