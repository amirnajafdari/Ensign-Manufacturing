    NB 250 Pipe Clips in Industry

    The NB 250 pipe clip is a two-piece bolted clip for 10" nominal bore pipe with an outside diameter of 273 mm. Two pressed strap halves wrap around the pipe and are drawn together with two hex bolts, holding it securely to a support, bracket, channel or steel section. Its clip bore (D) is 278 mm, made from 40 × 6 mm strap and fixed with M16 × 50 bolts and nuts, as shown in the dimension table above.

    At this size, pipe clips are typically used on large mains and headers such as cooling water, water treatment, process headers and large utility distribution. Choose stainless steel for food, pharmaceutical, marine or corrosive environments and for stainless pipework, galvanised for outdoor and general industrial use, and carbon steel where the clip will be painted with the steelwork.
