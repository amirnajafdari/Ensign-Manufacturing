    Fabricated Valves, Pipe Supports & Inspection Doors

    At Ensign Manufacturing we design and fabricate industrial components for process plants, bulk handling and site services. Based in Worksop, we supply fabricated valves, pipe support systems, inspection doors, spill pallets, diesel transport tanks and sliding doors to companies across Huddersfield, Cheshire, Yorkshire, Manchester and the rest of the UK.

    Choose the size and material you need, from SS316 stainless and S275 carbon steel to galvanised finishes, or send us your drawing and we'll quote for a custom build. Our team will help you choose the right product, and we're trusted by businesses such as Toyota, Veolia, Greif and the University of Sheffield.

    Ready to start your next project? Get in touch for a quote. We'll be glad to help.
