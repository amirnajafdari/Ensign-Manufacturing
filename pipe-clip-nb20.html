Pipe Clips NB 20

Description

  • Two-piece bolted pipe clip: two pressed strap halves with a formed saddle that wraps around the pipe.

  • Clamped with two hex bolts and nuts for a secure, adjustable grip.
  • Available in stainless steel, galvanised and carbon steel. Other pipe sizes on request.
